We often hear about the use of TF and TFE, but what about its use in large structure. Hear the story of OVHcloud, one of the biggest European hosting and cloud providers, with their challenges (large at-scale infra, security, self-provisioning via API) and the benefits. Was the choice to switch from an “homemade” solution to the migration of its teams to TFE easy?Horacio will tell you this story through figures, anecdotes, and internal feedback from their teams.
