I have been speaking and writing about web components for years, and last year in a conference somebody asked me a question that hurt: “After all this time, do you still believe in Web Components? React has won, man, and your Web Components will go down the way of Betamax…”. In this talk we will see that in 2019 Web Components are far from dead, they are part of the platform, used everywhere. The revolution has already come, but it’s a silent one. Let’s look at it together!
